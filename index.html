We don't believe the stars decide for you.

We do believe, though, that it's worth pausing now and then to see what the celestial clock is showing — not because everything is written from that point on, but because a good horoscope gives you better questions, not answers.

Astro Pucc speaks in two voices. One does its homework — on a planetary position, a lunar cycle, its measurable effects. The other doesn't sugarcoat it — it says out loud what everyone already thinks about their sign, but no one dares say to their face.

For now it's completely free, and it'll stay that way for as long as that lasts. Later there may be more to it — but that path will reveal itself on its own.
